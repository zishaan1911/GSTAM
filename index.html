Who we are

A community of scientists, engineers and educators applying green science to Malaysia's forests, coastlines, cities and industries.

Founded to bridge research and practice, GSTAM works with universities, government agencies and industry partners to translate environmental science into projects that reduce emissions, restore ecosystems and build technical capacity nationwide.

Where our work is concentrated

Five interconnected fields where Malaysian environmental challenges meet applied science.

01 Renewable Energy Solar deployment, grid resilience and technical training for Malaysia's energy transition. Read more
02 Recycling & Circular Economy Materials recovery, industrial symbiosis and policy work to close the waste loop. Read more
03 Biotechnology Applied research in bioremediation, biomaterials and tropical biodiversity. Read more
04 Ecosystem & Water Science Mangrove, forest and river-basin monitoring in partnership with local communities. Read more
05 Environmental Education Training programmes, school outreach and public science communication. Read more
