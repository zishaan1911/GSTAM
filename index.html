GreenNova Green Technology Club

Advancing technology
for a greener future.

GreenNova connects students, innovators and communities working across renewable energy, biotechnology, sustainability, and green technology.

Who we are

A community of students, innovators and educators exploring green technology to create practical solutions for a more sustainable future.

GreenNova brings together members who are passionate about sustainability, technology and innovation, turning ideas into projects that promote environmental awareness, develop technical skills and inspire meaningful action.

Where our work is concentrated

Five interconnected fields where technology, innovation and sustainability come together.

01 Renewable Energy Exploring solar technology, energy efficiency and innovative solutions for a cleaner energy future. Read more
02 Recycling & Circular Economy Promoting responsible resource use, recycling initiatives and practical approaches to reducing waste. Read more
03 Biotechnology Exploring biotechnology applications that support environmental protection, sustainable materials and biodiversity. Read more
04 Ecosystem & Water Science Raising awareness and developing initiatives focused on forests, waterways and the protection of natural ecosystems. Read more
05 Environmental Education Workshops, student activities and public outreach that make sustainability and green technology accessible to everyone. Read more
