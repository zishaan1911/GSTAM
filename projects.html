Projects & Initiatives

Fieldwork, not just findings.

A working archive of GSTAM's active and completed projects — where they happen, who they're with, and what they've produced.

Conservation

Matang Mangrove Carbon & Biodiversity Programme

PerakSince 2019Ongoing

Long-term monitoring of carbon sequestration and fisheries recovery across the Matang Mangrove Forest Reserve, run jointly with the Perak Forestry Department.

Renewable Energy

Community Solar Microgrids for East Coast Fishing Villages

Terengganu2022 – ongoing

Designing and piloting small-scale solar microgrids for coastal communities with unreliable grid access, paired with technician training delivered on-site.

Circular Economy

Materials Recovery Pilot with Selangor Municipal Councils

Selangor2021 – 2023Completed

A pilot programme testing household sorting incentives and improved sorting-line design across three municipal recovery facilities.

Biotechnology

Bioremediation of Palm Oil Mill Effluent

Johor2020 – ongoing

Screening native microbial strains for effluent treatment applications, in partnership with a regional palm oil processing cooperative.

Sustainable Agriculture

Low-Input Rice Trials, Sabah

Sabah2023 – ongoing

Field trials of water-efficient, reduced-fertiliser rice growing methods on working smallholder farms, run with state agriculture officers.

Environmental Science

River Basin Water Quality Monitoring Network

SabahSince 2018

A network of monitoring stations tracking sediment, nutrient and pollutant levels along the Kinabatangan river system.

Education

Green Schools & Field Learning Programme

NationwideSince 2016

Hands-on field science days for secondary schools, run at partner forest reserves and research stations across Malaysia.

Conservation

Habitat Corridor Mapping, Kinabatangan

Sabah2021 – ongoing

Mapping wildlife movement corridors where forest, plantation and settlement land intersect, to inform replanting priorities.
