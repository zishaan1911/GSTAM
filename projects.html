Projects & Initiatives

Ideas are better when they're built.

Explore GreenNova's projects and initiatives — where members experiment, collaborate and turn green technology ideas into practical experiences and solutions.

Conservation

GreenNova Mangrove Conservation Initiative

PerakOngoing

A student-led initiative exploring mangrove ecosystems, biodiversity and the importance of coastal conservation through field learning and environmental activities.

Renewable Energy

Student Solar Energy Project

MalaysiaOngoing

Exploring small-scale solar systems, renewable energy concepts and practical applications through student experiments, demonstrations and hands-on learning.

Circular Economy

Campus Recycling & Waste Reduction Initiative

CampusOngoing

A practical initiative focused on recycling awareness, responsible consumption and simple ways to reduce waste across student spaces and activities.

Biotechnology

Green Biotechnology Exploration

Student-ledOngoing

Exploring how biotechnology can contribute to environmental solutions, sustainable materials and cleaner processes through research, discussion and experimentation.

Sustainable Agriculture

Smart & Sustainable Agriculture Project

MalaysiaOngoing

Exploring smart farming, water efficiency and sustainable agricultural practices that can make food production more resource-conscious and resilient.

Environmental Science

Environmental Monitoring & Awareness Project

MalaysiaOngoing

Helping members understand environmental monitoring through activities focused on water, ecosystems, biodiversity and the role of data in understanding environmental change.

Education

Green Learning & Outreach Programme

CommunityOngoing

Workshops, talks and hands-on learning activities designed to make green technology and environmental sustainability accessible, practical and engaging.

Conservation

Biodiversity & Habitat Awareness Project

MalaysiaOngoing

Exploring Malaysia's biodiversity and natural habitats through awareness activities, educational content and conservation-focused student projects.
