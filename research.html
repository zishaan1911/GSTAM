Research themes

Four areas where GreenNova members can explore technology, sustainability and environmental challenges.

Renewable Energy

Exploring solar energy and smarter energy systems

Members investigate renewable energy technologies, solar deployment, energy efficiency and emerging approaches to building cleaner and more resilient energy systems.

Circular Economy

Finding better ways to reduce, reuse and recover

Projects explore recycling, responsible material use, waste reduction and circular approaches that can turn environmental challenges into opportunities for innovation.

Applied Biotechnology

Connecting biological science with sustainability

Members explore biotechnology applications in environmental protection, sustainable materials, bioremediation and other areas where biological systems can support greener solutions.

Environmental Science

Understanding ecosystems through science and data

Activities cover biodiversity, water quality, ecosystem monitoring and environmental observation, helping members understand how scientific evidence can guide sustainable decisions.

Featured project
Environmental science

Exploring the role of mangroves in protecting coastal ecosystems

A GreenNova learning and conservation initiative focused on mangrove ecosystems, biodiversity and the importance of coastal habitats. Members explore field observations, environmental awareness and practical conservation approaches.
