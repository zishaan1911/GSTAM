Research themes

Four areas where GSTAM members hold sustained, active research programmes.

Renewable Energy

Grid-scale solar performance in tropical conditions

Field monitoring of photovoltaic degradation, soiling and inverter performance under Malaysia's humidity and cloud cover, feeding into technical guidance for developers and grid planners.

Circular Economy

Materials recovery economics & industrial symbiosis

Cost-benefit studies of municipal recycling infrastructure, and mapping opportunities for one industry's waste stream to become another's feedstock.

Applied Biotechnology

Bioremediation & biomaterials from tropical microbes

Screening native microbial strains for pollutant breakdown and biodegradable-material applications, in partnership with university bioscience faculties.

Ecosystem & Water Science

Mangrove carbon stocks & river-basin health

Long-term plots tracking mangrove biomass and carbon sequestration, plus water-quality monitoring along river systems shared with fishing communities.

Featured study
Ecosystem & water science

Measuring carbon stocks across the Matang Mangrove Forest Reserve

A joint programme with university partners and the Perak state forestry department, tracking above- and below-ground carbon across managed and unmanaged mangrove plots. Nine years of plot data now underpin GSTAM's technical submissions on blue-carbon policy.

Read the technical summary
In the lab

Native microbes, tested for real industrial waste streams.

GSTAM-affiliated labs screen microbial strains sourced from Malaysian soils and wetlands for their ability to break down pollutants and industrial by-products — early-stage work that feeds into our biotechnology focus area.
