Focus areas

Eight fields, one working method: curiosity, learning, then action.

Each area below gives GreenNova members an opportunity to learn, experiment and build practical solutions. These are the fields where our members explore green technology and sustainability through projects, workshops and activities.

Turning waste into an opportunity for innovation.

03 — Biotechnology

Exploring biology as a pathway to sustainable innovation.

GreenNova members explore biotechnology applications ranging from environmental solutions and biomaterials to biodiversity and sustainable production, connecting biological science with real-world green technology ideas.

04
Environmental Science
Understanding our environment well enough to build something meaningful for it.

Our environmental science activities explore areas such as water quality, air quality, soil, ecosystems and biodiversity — helping members understand environmental challenges and how technology can contribute to solving them.

05 — Sustainable Agriculture

Modern agriculture faces growing challenges from resource use, changing weather and environmental pressures. GreenNova explores sustainable farming ideas that use resources more efficiently while supporting food production and environmental responsibility.

Members can explore areas such as smart agriculture, water efficiency, sustainable growing methods and agricultural technology through projects, demonstrations and collaborative learning.

06 — Green Engineering

Efficiency is a design decision, made early.

We explore energy-efficient systems, sustainable materials, smart technologies and practical engineering approaches that can reduce environmental impact without sacrificing function.

07 — Conservation

Protecting ecosystems through awareness, technology and action.

GreenNova promotes a stronger understanding of Malaysia's ecosystems and biodiversity, encouraging members to explore conservation technologies, environmental monitoring and community-led efforts to protect natural habitats.
