Focus areas

Eight fields, one working method: evidence, then action.

Each area below is led by its own committee, with dedicated field programmes and publications. This is not a menu of services — it's where GSTAM's members actually spend their time.

Designing waste out of Malaysian industry.

03 — Biotechnology

Tropical biodiversity as a scientific resource, not just a backdrop.

From bioremediation microbes to plant-derived biomaterials, GSTAM's biotech researchers work at the intersection of Malaysia's exceptional biodiversity and applied industrial science.

04
Environmental Science
Understanding a river, a reef or a forest well enough to tell someone what to actually do about it.

Our environmental science programme spans hydrology, air quality, soil science and biodiversity monitoring — grounded in long-term Malaysian field datasets rather than borrowed models.

Rice paddies, Donggongon, Sabah
05 — Sustainable Agriculture

Malaysian smallholders face rising input costs and increasingly erratic rainfall. GSTAM's agriculture programme trials low-input, water-efficient growing methods suited to local conditions — tested on working farms, not just research plots.

Current trials cover rice, cocoa and mixed vegetable systems, run in partnership with state agriculture departments and farmer cooperatives across Sabah and the northern peninsula.

06 — Green Engineering

Efficiency is a design decision, made early.

We work with engineering teams on energy-efficient building systems, low-carbon materials and industrial process redesign — the unglamorous work that determines most of a project's actual footprint.

07 — Conservation

Protecting habitat corridors as working landscapes.

GSTAM supports habitat-connectivity research along river systems where wildlife corridors intersect plantation and settlement land — work that has to account for people as much as species.

08 — Environmental Education

Science communicated is science that gets used.

GSTAM runs school outreach, public lecture series and technical workshops — translating field research into material that teachers, officials and community groups can actually use.
