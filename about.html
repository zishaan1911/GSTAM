About GreenNova

A creative home for students passionate about green technology.

GreenNova is a student-led club bringing together students, innovators and educators who are passionate about sustainability and technology. We exist to turn ideas into practical projects, build technical skills and inspire positive environmental action.

Mission

Turn green technology ideas into action students can build.

We explore, create and share practical ideas across green technology and environmental innovation — giving members opportunities to learn, experiment and develop solutions that can make a real difference.

Vision

A community where technology and sustainability grow together.

We want environmental thinking and technological creativity to become a natural part of student life — encouraging the next generation to build a cleaner, smarter and more sustainable future.

Objectives

What we aim to do, in practice.

  • Explore renewable energy, biotechnology, recycling and other areas of green technology.
  • Provide hands-on learning opportunities through workshops, projects and technical activities.
  • Encourage students to develop innovative solutions to environmental challenges.
  • Build collaboration between students, educators, industry and community partners.
  • Promote environmental awareness through events, campaigns and public engagement.

Our Journey

From a student community with a shared interest in sustainability to a growing green technology club.

2022

A group of students begins exploring green technology together.

Students with shared interests in sustainability, engineering and technology begin meeting to exchange ideas and discuss practical environmental solutions.

2023

GreenNova is established as a student club.

The club is formally organised to create a space for students to collaborate, learn new technologies and develop sustainability-focused projects.

2024

First green technology workshops and activities.

GreenNova begins organising hands-on sessions, awareness activities and collaborative projects focused on renewable energy, recycling and sustainability.

2025

Projects and collaborations expand.

Members take on larger projects and begin connecting with external communities, organisations and partners interested in green innovation.

2026

GreenNova today.

GreenNova continues to grow as a community for students interested in green technology, sustainability, innovation and meaningful environmental action.

Our Team

GreenNova is led by a student committee that coordinates activities, projects and member engagement.

President

Provides overall direction and represents GreenNova in collaborations and official activities.

Vice President

Supports club leadership and oversees activities, programmes and project coordination.

Secretary

Manages club records, communications, scheduling and administrative matters.

Treasurer

Oversees club finances, budgeting and funding for activities and projects.

Project Lead

Coordinates technical projects, competitions and hands-on green technology initiatives.

Events & Outreach Lead

Leads workshops, campaigns, community activities and public engagement.

GreenNova's student committee works together to plan activities, support members and grow the club's impact through practical initiatives.

Working together

Ideas are only useful when people bring them to life.

GreenNova encourages members to learn by doing — working together on projects, workshops and environmental activities that turn green technology concepts into practical experiences.
